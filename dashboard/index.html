How the value is set
  • Multiple. Median market cap / TVL of the live-token protocols in the same category with at least $10M of TVL, each ratio capped at 3.0, shrunk toward the market median, and clamped to 0.02 to 1.50. A category with fewer than 3 such names takes the market median instead.
  • Growth. 1 + 0.4 × 7-day TVL change, change clamped to -50% to +100%.
  • Maturity. 0.70 + 0.30 × age / 3y. Longer-running protocols without a token count for more.
  • Breadth. 1 + 0.04 per extra chain, capped at 4 extra chains.

What no token means here. A protocol is listed as pre-token when its DefiLlama record carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol. That is the record, not proof: a protocol on this board may still have a token its record does not carry. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are left out, as are versioned pools and exchanges.

What the implied value is. A model’s claim about what a token would be worth if the protocol’s record stayed as it is. It does not predict whether or when a token is issued, and it is not investment advice. Farm steps name what a position costs and how long it takes; they never promise an airdrop.
